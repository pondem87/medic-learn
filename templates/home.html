About us

Connecting health professionals with specialist knowledge

MedEd Botswana brings continuing medical education (CME) to doctors, nurses, and other health professionals. Learners will be able to access training from various specialists and earn continuing professional development (CPD) points as they grow their knowledge.

For trainers and specialists, the platform offers a way to distribute learning content with greater reach and lower costs. Share your expertise and help more health professionals continue their development.
